Infinitunes design directions

One final unified prototype, plus the four earlier explorations it was built from. Each covers home, browse, charts, album, artist, playlist, song, podcast, search, library, settings and auth. Resize the window below 768px for the mobile layout.

Final directionUnified Liquid GlassBest of A to D in one system: labeled sidebar (A), artwork-lit headers and floating player (B), docked queue and compact table density (C), quick picks, dock and sheets on phones (D). Liquid Glass materials, spring physics, and a full theme customizer in Settings > Appearance. Includes a route map for implementation.

Earlier explorations

Variant ALibrary SidebarRefined classic: labeled left sidebar, slim top bar, full-width player. Mobile bottom tabs. Auth as a modal. Variant BEditorial Top NavNo sidebar: top tabs, hero banner, artwork-tinted headers, floating player. Mobile drawer. Auth as a split page. Variant CCompact Desktop AppDense: icon rail, tables, queue pane on the right, 28-32px controls. Mobile top tabs only. Auth as a minimal card. Variant DSearch-first DockMobile-app feel: search hero, quick picks, floating bottom dock and player pill. Auth as a bottom sheet.
