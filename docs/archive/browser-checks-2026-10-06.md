## Defects

- **D1 (P3, fixed `172a631`)**: the expanded player sheet rendered two overlapping `Close` buttons (`right-2` and `right-3`, 4 px apart), drawing a doubled X and exposing two "Close" controls to assistive tech. Removed the second one. Test: `tests/dom/expanded-player.test.tsx` (fails with 2 before, 1 after).
- **D2 (P2, not fixed)**: raw `&amp;` in search song rows (title and subtitle). Cause is the song-list render path not calling `decode()` on `title`/`subtitle`; AGENTS.md says raw upstream strings are still HTML-encoded. Proposed: decode in the song-list row (worker K owns `components/song-list`), plus a test with an `&amp;` title.
- **D3 (P3, not fixed)**: long song-row titles clip without an ellipsis at 390 (hypothesis, not isolated: the title is an inline link inside a `w-full truncate` parent that is not `min-w-0` in its flex row). Proposed: `min-w-0` on the text column and `block truncate` on the link (UI-50, worker K).
- **D4 (P3, not fixed)**: light theme, destructive text on its tinted background is 3.97:1 (Logout on `/me/*`, Delete Account on `/settings`), below 4.5:1. `packages/ui` is read-only; proposed: override the colour app-side (for example `text-red-700`) on those two buttons or darken the `--destructive` token in light mode.
- **D5 (P3, not fixed)**: at 390 the navbar Languages (40x40) and user menu (32x32) buttons and the detail-header Like/Download/More options (32x32) and Play (32px high) are below 44px (DV-8 asked for 44px targets in the player sheet, queue and footer, which do pass). Proposed: app-side `size-11` on those controls at small widths.
- **D6 (P3, not fixed)**: the page headings in light mode render with a heavy `drop-shadow-md` ("Settings", "Account Settings", "Create an Account"), visibly smudging the text (`ui-settings-1280-light.png`). Proposed: drop the shadow in light mode.
