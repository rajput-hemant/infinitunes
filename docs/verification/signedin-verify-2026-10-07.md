Branch `task/signedin-verify` (from `migration/bun-monorepo` at `a671298`).

Setup: a throwaway `postgres:18.6-alpine` container on `127.0.0.1:3111` (migrated with `bun run db:migrate`),
`next dev` on port 3211 under Node with `NODE_OPTIONS=--openssl-legacy-provider`, the main checkout's
`.env.local` copied in with `DATABASE_URL`, `AUTH_URL` and `NEXT_PUBLIC_APP_URL` pointed at the local pair,
rate limiting off and no Resend key. Real JioSaavn upstream (reachable). Browser: chrome-devtools-axi (named
session), real signup through the form, 390x844 touch emulation and 1280x800, light and dark through
`prefers-color-scheme`. Forced colors came from a separate headless Chrome 155 driven over CDP
(`Emulation.setEmulatedMedia`), because chrome-devtools-axi has no forced-colors switch. The container, the env
copy and the servers were removed or stopped afterwards.

## Observations, not fixed

- Settings pages have many controls under 44 px at 390 (row 8); D5 was scoped to library and playlist pages.
- `loginSchema` reuses `passwordSchema`, so the login form rejects a password lacking an uppercase letter, digit or symbol before any request (`AGENTS.md` calls `loginSchema` email-only). Accounts with older, weaker passwords would be blocked client-side.
- Bottom-nav tab order at 390 (row 10).
